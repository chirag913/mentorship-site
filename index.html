Real questions people ask before applying

Before you ask

Is dropshipping even still viable in 2026?＋

Yes — but the "pick any product, run one ad" era is over. What works now is proper product research, real creative testing, and treating it like a business with margins and retention, not a lottery ticket. That's exactly what mentorship teaches.

Should I do Indian or international dropshipping?＋

Depends on your budget, risk tolerance and payment access — both are covered in your intake, and we decide together in your first conversation rather than guessing upfront.

I have a small budget for ads. Can I still start?＋

You need some capital for testing — dropshipping isn't free. But you don't need a huge budget to start testing properly if the process is right. We'll be honest with you about what's realistic for your specific number.

Why isn't mentorship pricing shown on the site?＋

Someone starting from zero needs a different depth of involvement than someone scaling an existing store — so it's discussed directly on WhatsApp once I understand your situation, not guessed at with a generic price tag.

What if I'm not ready for full mentorship yet?＋

Start with the ₹2,499 clarity call. One focused session on your store or your plan — and if you join mentorship later, you'll already know how I work.
