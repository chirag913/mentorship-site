Questions

What people ask before applying.

Something else? Ask me on WhatsApp.

Why does mentorship start at ₹49,999?

Because it's ongoing 1-on-1 work on your actual business — the week-one audit, weekly reviews of your numbers, and direct access to me — not a course. The exact scope depends on where you're starting from, and we agree it on WhatsApp before you pay. Ad spend, stock and tools are separate business costs.

What's the difference between the clarity call and mentorship?

The ₹2,499 clarity call is one 45-minute session on one specific problem — you leave with a clear next move. Mentorship is an ongoing working relationship: I review your store and numbers every week and you can reach me directly in between.

Is dropshipping still viable in 2026?

Yes — but the "pick any product, run one ad" era is over. What works now is proper product research, real creative testing, and treating it like a business with margins and retention. That's what we work on.

Should I do Indian or international dropshipping?

It depends on your budget, risk tolerance and payment access. Both are covered in your intake, and we decide together in the first conversation rather than guessing upfront.

I have a small budget for ads. Can I still start?

You need some capital for testing — dropshipping isn't free. But you don't need a huge budget if the process is right. I'll be honest about what's realistic for your number. If mentorship isn't the right commitment yet, the clarity call is a sensible place to start.

Do you guarantee results?

No. Nobody honest can promise an income number. What I commit to is real, direct support — and if I'm not giving you that while you're implementing, I extend your mentorship at no extra cost until I do.
