Where it usually breaks

Most stores don't fail from lack of effort. They fail from not knowing which thing is broken.

  1. You've launched three or four products, none of them hit — and you can't tell if it was the product, the creative or the targeting.

  2. Your Meta or TikTok ad account got flagged or banned, and you're rebuilding with no idea how to avoid it next time.

  3. COD return rates are quietly eating every rupee of margin, and there's no system to fix it.

    India

  4. You can't get a reliable payment gateway, or your Shopify payouts keep getting held.

    International

  5. You're learning from videos and courses filmed years ago — and the platforms have changed since.

  6. Nobody who actually knows what they're doing has looked at your store and told you the truth.

If two of these are you, that's what mentorship is for.

How mentorship works

Not lessons. Working sessions on your actual business.

Every week follows the same loop. It's simple on purpose. The value is an experienced operator looking at the real thing.

  1. 01

    You bring it

    Your store, product, ad account and numbers — plus whatever's stuck right now.

  2. 02

    We diagnose

    We go through it together and find what's actually broken: product, creative, targeting, backend or margin.

  3. 03

    We decide

    One clear set of changes, in priority order. Not twenty ideas.

  4. 04

    You implement

    You do the work. When something doesn't make sense, you message me directly.

  5. 05

    We review

    The numbers come back and we read them together — then decide the next move.

Then again. It starts with a full store, product and ad-account audit in week one.

Questions

What people ask before applying.

Something else? Ask me on WhatsApp.

Is dropshipping still viable in 2026?

Yes — but the "pick any product, run one ad" era is over. What works now is proper product research, real creative testing, and treating it like a business with margins and retention. That's what we work on.

Should I do Indian or international dropshipping?

It depends on your budget, risk tolerance and payment access. Both are covered in your intake, and we decide together in the first conversation rather than guessing upfront.

I have a small budget for ads. Can I still start?

You need some capital for testing — dropshipping isn't free. But you don't need a huge budget if the process is right. I'll be honest with you about what's realistic for your specific number.

What does the ₹49,000 cover?

Direct 1-on-1 mentorship: the week-one audit of your store, product and ad account, a roadmap for your market, weekly reviews of your numbers, and direct access to me for course-correction. Ad spend, stock and tools are separate business costs. We talk on WhatsApp and confirm it's the right fit before you pay.

What if I'm not ready for full mentorship yet?

Start with the ₹2,499 clarity call. One focused session on your store or your plan — and if you join mentorship later, you'll already know how I work.

Do you guarantee results?

No. Nobody honest can promise an income number. What I commit to is real, direct support — and if I'm not giving you that while you're implementing, I extend your mentorship at no extra cost until I do.
