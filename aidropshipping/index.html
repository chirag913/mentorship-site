I've never done dropshipping. Is this okay?

Yes — it's built for beginners. Week 1 starts from setting up your store, and every week has one clear task, so you always know what to do next.

Do I need to be good with AI?

No. You get the prompts ready to copy and paste, and the lessons show exactly where to use them.

Can I sell internationally from India?

Yes. Week 4 covers suppliers, shipping times and payment gateways that work for sellers based in India — the part most people get stuck on.

How much do I need besides the ₹7,999?

You'll need a small budget for your store (Shopify and a domain) and your first ad tests. We go through exactly what to spend, and when, in Week 1 — so you don't waste money before you're ready.

Is it live or recorded?

The lessons are recorded so you can watch at your own pace. Each week there's a group session on Google Meet or a recorded walkthrough answering the batch's questions, and the WhatsApp group runs throughout.

How do I get access after paying?

After payment, message me on WhatsApp (the button on the confirmation page does it for you). I'll add you to the cohort group and send your Week 1 task.

Will I definitely make money?

No, and I won't pretend otherwise. I'll teach you the process I use and give you the tools to do it faster. Results depend on your product, your budget and how consistently you do the work.
