The idea

Not a tourist trip. Just a few people building ambitious things.

Founders’ Table is a small, private gathering in the mountains for people who are actually building. A few days away from the noise to share what is working, fix what is stuck, and sit with people who think at the same level.

The room is deliberately small, so every person in it matters. That is why every seat is handpicked.

Questions

Good to know.

Is this a course or a workshop?

No. It is a small group of founders in one place for a few days, with honest conversations about what you are building. No slides, no selling.

When exactly is it?

We are planning for mid November. Exact dates are shared with selected founders.

What does it cost?

The contribution is ₹25,000 to 30,000 per person, and it covers your stay and food. Full details come with your invitation.

Will I get a private room?

Rooms are finalised once we know the group size and everyone’s preference, private or shared. Tell us yours in the application.

How do I get there?

Getting to Manali is on you: fly to Kullu airport (Bhuntar) or take a bus to Manali bus stand. If you need a pickup from either, we can arrange it. Just tell us in advance, once your payment is confirmed. Selected founders get the exact meeting point.

How are people selected?

We read every application and pick people who are building seriously and can add value to the group. Shortlisted applicants get a short call first.

What if I am not selected?

You will still hear from us, and you will be the first to know about the next one.
