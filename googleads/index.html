Overview

Why Google Ads over the usual playbook

Most dropshipping advice defaults to Meta because that's where the store you're copying started. But Meta sells to people who weren't looking for anything. Google Ads puts your product in front of someone who already typed "buy" into a search bar. That's a completely different — and cheaper — kind of customer to convert.

Google Ads — Intent

  • Person is actively searching to solve a problem or buy a product
  • You compete on relevance and offer, not just scroll-stopping creative
  • Shopping ads show price + image right in the results — pre-qualifies the click
  • Slower to blow up, but cheaper to sustain once it works

Meta / TikTok — Interruption

  • Person wasn't looking for your product — you have to create the want
  • Wins live and die on creative testing volume and video production
  • Higher top-of-funnel reach, much lower baseline purchase intent
  • Cheaper to test fast, but algorithm fatigue kills winners quickly

The sequence

Setup, in the order it actually has to happen

This is the process — not a summary of it. Each step depends on the one before it, which is exactly why most people who skip ahead end up with a Shopping campaign and no idea why it's not spending.

Pick a product with provable search demand

Before you touch Shopify, open Google Trends and Keyword Planner. You're looking for a product people already search to solve a problem — not something you saw go viral. Viral proves attention exists on social; it proves nothing about search intent.

Tool: Keyword Planner, Google Trends

Build a store that answers the search, not just "sells"

Single-product Shopify page, above-the-fold answer to the exact question someone searched, real reviews, fast load time. If your page doesn't match what the person typed, Google will quietly punish your Quality Score and your cost-per-click climbs.

Set up Merchant Center and launch a Shopping campaign

This is usually the first campaign to run because Shopping ads carry the lowest friction — price and image are visible before the click. Get your product feed approved, title and description optimized for the actual search terms from step one, and start with Google's Smart Bidding off.

Start: Manual CPC or Maximize Clicks

Layer in a Search campaign on your highest-intent terms

Separate campaign, exact-match keywords only for the first two weeks — no broad match. You want data on the terms that convert before you let Google's algorithm interpret what "similar" means. Write ad copy that mirrors the search term, not generic "Shop Now" language.

Set a budget you can survive losing for two weeks

Realistic starting range is ₹1,000–₹2,000/day. This isn't a guess — it's what it takes to get enough clicks to reach statistical significance on which keywords and products actually convert. Anything less and you're optimizing on noise.

Install conversion tracking before you spend a rupee more

GA4 plus native Google Ads conversion tracking, tied to actual purchases — not "add to cart." Without this, every optimization decision you make afterward is a guess dressed up as a strategy.

Non-negotiable before scaling

Run the weekly cut-and-scale cycle

Every week: kill keywords and products spending without converting, push budget toward the ones that are. This is the actual job of running the account — the setup is one afternoon, the optimization is the business.

Where people lose the budget

The five mistakes I see on repeat

✕ Broad match on day one

Lets Google spend your budget on loosely related searches before you know which exact terms convert.

✕ No negative keyword list

Without it, you're paying for clicks from people searching "free," "jobs," or "reviews" who were never buying.

✕ Judging results before 15–30 conversions

Turning off a campaign after three days of no sales is reading a coin flip as a pattern.

✕ Store built for the ad, not the search

If the landing page doesn't answer what the person typed, Quality Score drops and every click gets more expensive.

✕ Scaling budget and creative at the same time

Change one variable at a time, or you'll never know which decision actually moved the number.

✕ No conversion tracking, ever

Running ads on vibes. You'll feel busy and stay unprofitable at the same time.
